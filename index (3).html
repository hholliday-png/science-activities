The bond energy recipe

  1. List the bonds in the reactants and count each kind.
  2. Multiply: count × bond energy.
  3. Add them up. That is energy in.
  4. Do the same for the products. That is energy out.
  5. ΔH = energy in − energy out.

Example: 4 C–H bonds at 413 each → 4 × 413 = 1,652 kJ.

Heat: q = mcΔT

qheat energy, in joules (J)
mmass, in grams (g)
cspecific heat; water is 4.18 J/g·°C
ΔTfinal temp − starting temp

50 g of water, 20 °C → 30 °C. ΔT = 10. q = 50 × 4.18 × 10 = 2,090 J.

Smaller minus bigger is always negative: 671 − 854 = −183.
